## 👋 Intro

<b>Pro DoubleClick</b> is a lightweight Chrome extension (MV3 compliant), provides an incredibly convenient way to quickly open multiple webpages without having to manually `ctrl + click` or `ctrl + shift + click` link. Improve your workflow; just Double Click to open links in a new tab (front or background). 

Designed with minimalist UI yet packed with customization. Save time, maximize your productivity and experience fluid browsing with Pro DoubleClick!

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

> [!CAUTION]
> Your browser may display pop up warning after installing Pro DoubleClick, it is just a standard precaution message; you can ignore that.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Pro DoubleClick never collects user data, browsing history, or click behavior. Your activity stays entirely yours. As a pure utility extension, its sole purpose is to streamline user productivity.

> [!NOTE]
> Pro DoubleClick requires below mentioned permissions:
>
>`tabs` essential for opening links in a new tab (front or background)
>
>`activeTab` gives the extension temporary access to the currently active tab
>
>`storage` used to save user site‑specific preferences and settings locally
